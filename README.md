# 📊 Insurance and Policy Analytics Dashboard

## 🧾 Project Overview
This project contains two interactive and data-driven dashboards — **Policy Analytics Dashboard** and **Insurance Analytics Dashboard** — developed using **Excel | Power BI | SQL **.  
The dashboards are designed to analyze **policy performance, claim patterns, customer distribution, and sales achievements** in the insurance sector.  
It provides an end-to-end understanding of business performance and helps decision-makers track growth, performance, and key KPIs visually.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- To analyze **policy count, claim data, and premium trends** using interactive visualizations.  
- To monitor **executive performance**, including meeting counts, revenue funnel, and achievement metrics.  
- To help identify **high-performing policy types, customer segments, and revenue opportunities**.  
- To present a **real-time view of insurance operations** for management and analytics teams.

---

## 🧠 Dashboards Included

### 🟢 1. Policy Analytics Dashboard
This dashboard focuses on **policy-related metrics** such as policy count, claim statistics, and customer data.

**Key KPIs:**
- 🧾 **Policy Count:** 5000  
- 💬 **Total Claims:** 1712  
- 👥 **Customer Count:** 5000  
- 💰 **Total Claim Amount:** ₹86 Million  
- ⏳ **Policies Expired This Year:** 526  

**Visual Insights:**
- **Policy Count by Age Group:** Shows how policy ownership varies across age segments (18–90 years).  
- **Policy Count by Gender:** Compares policies held by Male, Female, and Other genders.  
- **Policy Count by Policy Type:** Analyzes Health, Property, Life, and Auto policies.  
- **Monthly Premium Trend:** Displays the total premium collected across months, highlighting seasonal variations.  

---

### 🔵 2. Insurance Analytics Dashboard
This dashboard highlights **sales performance, executive achievements, and revenue funnel** data.

**Key KPIs:**
- 🔁 **Cross-sell Achievement:** 62.96%  
- 🧾 **Cross-sell Invoice Achievement:** 15.14%  
- 📦 **New Policy Achievement:** 17.44%  
- 💵 **Renewal Achievement:** 150.1%  
- 📉 **Renewal Invoice Achievement:** 68.14%

**Visual Insights:**
- **Meetings by Account Executive:** Shows the number of client meetings conducted for cross-sell, new, and renewal deals.  
- **Invoices by Account Executive:** Highlights the top performers in invoice generation.  
- **Opportunity by Revenue (Top 4):** Displays the top revenue-generating policies — Fire, DB-Mega Policy, EL-Group Mediclaim, and CVP GMC.  
- **Stage Funnel by Revenue:** Tracks revenue generation stages — Qualify, Negotiate, and Propose Solution.  
- **Product Distribution:** Shows business distribution across specialty categories like Liability, Marine, Employee Benefits, etc.  
- **Yearly Meeting Count:** Compares meetings between 2019 and 2020.

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- The **26–45 age group** holds the highest number of policies, indicating a strong working-age customer base.  
- **Health and Property policies** are the most popular policy types.  
- Monthly premium collection fluctuates slightly, peaking in **June and December**.  
- **Cross-sell and renewal performance** show high achievement rates, suggesting customer loyalty and sales efficiency.  
- **Top executives (e.g., Gilbert, Abhinav Shivam)** drive most meetings and revenue conversions.
