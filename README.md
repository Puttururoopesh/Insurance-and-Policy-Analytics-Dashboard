# Insurance-and-Policy-Analytics-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard that visualizes key insurance and policy metrics, including policy distribution, claim performance, revenue funnel, and executive achievements. Designed to help insurance companies make data-driven decisions and track performance efficiently
# 📊 Insurance & Policy Analytics Dashboard

This project showcases two interactive dashboards built for visualizing and analyzing **insurance and policy performance metrics**.  
The dashboards were created using **Power BI , SQL , Excel**.

---

## 🧠 Dashboards Overview

### 1️⃣ Policy Analytics Dashboard
**Highlights:**
- Total Policy Count: 5000  
- Total Claims: 1712  
- Total Claim Amount: ₹86M  
- Policies Expired This Year: 526  

**Key Insights:**
- Policy distribution by age group (18–90)
- Gender-wise and type-wise policy analysis  
- Monthly premium trend visualization  

### 2️⃣ Insurance Analytics Dashboard
**Highlights:**
- Cross-sell & renewal achievements
- Meeting and invoice analysis by account executive  
- Opportunity funnel by revenue  
- Yearly meeting trends and top-performing products  

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
- Power BI / Excel  
- Data Cleaning in Excel / Python  
- DAX & Power Query (if applicable)

---

## 🏁 Project Goal
To provide a visual understanding of:
- How insurance and policy performance varies by category  
- Which teams or executives perform best in sales and renewals  
- The trend of claims and revenue over time
